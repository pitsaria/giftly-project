Our Story

Wrapped with intention, sent with love.

Giftly began with one badly-wrapped present and a simple conviction: the way a gift arrives is part of the gift. It started at a kitchen table with a glue gun, a roll of ribbon, and a handful of orders from friends who kept asking where we got our wrapping done.

Word spread faster than we expected, and the kitchen table became a small studio — the one pictured below — where every box is still cut, folded and tied by hand. As orders grew past what any of us could track on paper, we built the Giftly app so customers could put together their own box, follow it from checkout to doorstep, and never forget an occasion again.

We're still a small team today, but the conviction hasn't changed: the way a gift arrives is part of the gift.
